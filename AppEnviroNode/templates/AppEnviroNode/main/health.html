Who's At Risk?

Children & Elderly

More susceptible to respiratory issues from poor air quality.

Respiratory Conditions

People with asthma or COPD are highly sensitive to PM2.5.

Outdoor Workers

Prolonged exposure increases health risks significantly.

Pregnant Women

High pollution can affect fetal development and maternal health.

Protective Measures

  • Wear N95 masks outdoors during high AQI.
  • Use HEPA air purifiers indoors.
  • Keep windows closed on polluted days.
  • Check AQI before exercising outside.

Symptoms to Watch

  • Persistent coughing or wheezing.
  • Eye, nose, and throat irritation.
  • Shortness of breath during light activity.
  • Unusual fatigue or headaches.

When to Seek Help

Seek immediate medical attention if you or someone else experiences severe symptoms.

Emergency Signs:

  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Chest pain or tightness
  • Severe dizziness or confusion
