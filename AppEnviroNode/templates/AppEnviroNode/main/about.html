Breathing Better in Taguig

EnviroNode is a smart city initiative designed to deliver real-time, accessible, and highly accurate air quality data, empowering the citizens of Taguig City with timely information to promote healthier living, informed decision-making, and environmental awareness.

Our Mission

To empower communities with actionable environmental data, promoting public health awareness and enabling data-driven policy making for a cleaner, greener city.

Our Vision

A future where every citizen has access to transparent environmental information, fostering a culture of sustainability and proactive health management.

How It Works

1. Data Collection

IoT sensors deployed across barangays continuously monitor pollutants and weather conditions.

2. Processing & AI

Data is securely transmitted, validated, and analyzed using machine learning algorithms.

3. Public Access

Information is presented through this intuitive dashboard for citizens and policymakers.

Our Client

Barangay Pembo, Taguig City

EnviroNode was developed in partnership with Barangay Pembo, Taguig City, to provide residents with accessible and transparent air quality information for their community. This collaboration represents our commitment to empowering local governments with data-driven environmental monitoring solutions.
