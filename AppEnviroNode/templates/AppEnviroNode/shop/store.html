The U-Node Architecture

A comprehensive breakdown of our custom-engineered environmental monitoring hardware. Built for precision, scalability, and real-time data transmission.

ESP8266 Microcontroller

The core processing unit. Handles I2C bus connections and manages seamless Wi-Fi data transmission to the central EnviroPlus servers.

BME680 Sensor

A state-of-environment sensor delivering highly accurate readings for ambient temperature, relative humidity, and barometric pressure.

SGP40 & MQ Series

Advanced gas and VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) sensor arrays designed to detect harmful air pollutants and calculate accurate air quality indexes.
